[licenses/Soccermatics-MIT.txt](licenses/Soccermatics-MIT.txt).

## StatsBomb

Event data, derived event selections in `data/`, and data visualisations originate
from [StatsBomb Open Data](https://github.com/hudl/open-data). Consult the provider's
[licence](https://github.com/hudl/open-data/blob/master/LICENSE.pdf) for permitted use
and attribution. This repository grants no additional rights to those data.
The StatsBomb logos in `assets/` and `web/assets/` remain provider marks, excluded
from the root MIT licence. No endorsement or club affiliation is implied.
Raw event files are downloaded locally, not bundled in Git.
