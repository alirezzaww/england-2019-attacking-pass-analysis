Probability, outcome and calibration

Predicted xG: a model's estimated chance of a goal. Outcome: recorded Goal = 1 or non-goal = 0. One outcome does not reveal that shot's true scoring probability.

Group diagnostic: compare mean predicted probability with goals / shots for similar predictions. Keep counts and group boundaries visible. A zero observed goal rate is not proof of zero chance.

Overall goal totals can agree while probability bands differ. Error scores compare probability predictions with outcomes; they do not alone certify calibration or practical usefulness. Fresh representative data and appropriate uncertainty assessment remain necessary.
