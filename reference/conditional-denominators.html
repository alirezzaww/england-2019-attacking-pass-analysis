Football analytics reference

Match the denominator to the question.

Location mix: completed passes from one zone / all the player's eligible completed passes.

Within-zone qualifying share: qualifying passes from that zone / eligible completed passes from that same zone.

Completion percentage: completed passes / attempted passes. We do not calculate this in lesson 20.

For pooled shares, add the numerators and denominators separately, then divide. An unweighted mean of zone percentages can produce a different result. A zero denominator is undefined; a zero numerator with a positive denominator is 0%.

Use a common zone definition and the same pass filters for numerator and denominator. Starting location is not pass destination or final-third entry. Comparing within broad zones does not fully adjust roles or difficulty.
