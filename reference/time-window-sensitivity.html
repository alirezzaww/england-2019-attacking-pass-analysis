Change one definition. Recheck the conclusion.

  1. State the original selection rule.
  2. Choose reasonable alternatives before interpreting their results.
  3. Keep data, other filters and denominators fixed.
  4. Check nested sets, duplicates, totals and player rates.
  5. Describe which findings persist and which depend on the choice.

For our inclusive windows: selection(5) ⊆ selection(10) ⊆ selection(15). More selected passes at 15 seconds is expected. It does not validate that choice. Sensitivity is not a confidence interval, significance test or proof of causality.
